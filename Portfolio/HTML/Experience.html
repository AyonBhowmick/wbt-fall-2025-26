University Management System

Developed a University Management System using Java, integrating File I/O and GUI design for efficient management of student and teacher records. Implemented a secure login and sign-up system for students, teachers, and admins. The system handles student records, section-wise student counts, tuition fees, and faculty management. Included a dynamic Fee Structure module for managing tuition, scholarships, and due payments. Enabled viewing and updating of student and teacher details through structured File I/O operations, all within an intuitive user interface.

Fund Raiser Management System

Introducing the Fundraiser Management System (FMS), an innovative solution tailored to streamline the complexities of organizing and executing fundraising campaigns. FMS serves as the cornerstone for individuals, charities, and organizations alike, offering a comprehensive suite of tools to orchestrate successful fundraising endeavors. With its intuitive interface and robust features, FMS simplifies campaign management, empowering users to set goals, define strategies, and track progress with ease. From DONER management and online fundraising to event planning and reporting, FMS provides a centralized platform to efficiently coordinate every aspect of the fundraising process. By harnessing the power of data analytics and engagement tools, FMS enables users to cultivate meaningful relationships with DONERs, optimize resource allocation, and measure the impact of their initiatives. With FMS, organizations can navigate the intricacies of fundraising with confidence, driving greater efficiency, effectiveness.
